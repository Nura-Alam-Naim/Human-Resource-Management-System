# 🚀 The Grand 11-Phase HRMS to SaaS Master Plan

This is the definitive roadmap for transforming the Leave Management Portal into a complete, enterprise-grade **Human Resource Management System (HRMS)**, culminating in a multi-tenant SaaS application.

---

## 🏗️ The 11 Phases of Evolution

### ✅ Completed Phases
1. **Phase 1: Core Organization & Hierarchy** *(Completed)*
   - Database Normalization (Departments & Designations).
   - Dynamic Org Chart & Global Transfers.
   - Role-based access and manager assignment.
2. **Phase 2: Time & Attendance** *(Completed)*
   - Interactive Web Clock-In widget.
   - Timesheet generation and HR attendance monitoring.
   - Average daily hours calculations.
3. **Phase 3: Advanced Leave Management** *(Completed)*
   - Weekend & Public Holiday exclusion logic.
   - Multi-tier approvals (Manager -> HR Admin).
   - Automated monthly leave accrual engine.
4. **Phase 4: System Reliability & Seeding** *(Completed)*
   - Database mock data seeding for immediate visual impact.
   - Data consistency safeguards.
5. **Phase 5: Employee Profiles & Document Management** *(Completed)*
   - Secure uploading of medical certificates, resumes, and ID cards.
   - Profile picture standardization and local storage integration.
6. **Phase 6: Payroll & Salary Calculations** *(Completed)*
   - Automated payslip generation based on attendance and leaves.
   - Base salary and deductions logic.

### 📍 Current & Upcoming Phases
7. **Phase 7: Internal Chat & Messaging** *(Current Phase)*
   - Direct messaging between employees, managers, and HR within the portal.
   - Read receipts and unread notifications.
8. **Phase 8: Recruitment & ATS (Applicant Tracking System)**
   - External job board for applicants.
   - Interview scheduling and candidate pipeline management.
9. **Phase 9: Performance Reviews & KPIs**
   - Manager grading systems and 360-degree feedback.
   - Goal tracking and automated review cycles.
10. **Phase 10: Asset Management**
    - Tracking company laptops, monitors, keys, and software licenses assigned to employees.
11. **Phase 11: The SaaS Transformation (Multi-Tenancy)**
    - Upgrading the database to support multiple isolated companies (tenants).
    - Subdomain routing (e.g., `companyA.hrms.com`).
    - Super-admin billing and subscription management (Stripe integration).
